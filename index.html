From brief to final cut

You sign off on the story before a single shot is generated.

  1. Brief and hook

    We agree on the audience, the platform and the one idea the short has to land.

  2. Script and beat board

    A written script and a shot-by-shot beat board for your approval.

  3. Characters and world

    Character sheets and style frames, locked so every shot matches.

  4. Generate and animate

    AI generation for the shots, Blender for anything that needs control, hand fixes where it drifts.

  5. Edit, sound, captions

    Cut to the beat, sound design and captions, exported in every ratio you post.
