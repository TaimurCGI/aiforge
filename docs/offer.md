# Offer

Set by Taimur on 2026-10-17, using the six steps from Tom Noske's post (instagram.com/p/DeSo34eBhvZ): niche, person, result, offer, price, content.

## 1. Niche

Story-first AI animated ads for brands that sell online. No single market: past clients are Ketro (pain relief cream), JTX (trading app) and CheerMyRun (marathon cheer app). Any product works, except ones that sell on sex appeal.

## 2. Who

The owner or head of creative at an ecom or app brand that runs paid social, often posting "hiring AI video editor" on X.
- Wants: winning creatives, fast, to scale spend.
- Fears: AI slop, wasted generation credits, fake portfolios.
- Why Taimur: seven years as a 3D animator, so he catches bad timing and off-model frames; 70 reels in one week for CheerMyRun; 130K organic views on one reel, 350K+ across the campaign.

## 3. Result

Ready-to-test, story-driven AI ads that hold attention, delivered fast. Proof: 130K organic on the top CheerMyRun reel, no ad spend. No promise of a winner or ROAS.

## 4. Offer

- **$50 per video**, up to 60 seconds.
- Includes research, script and production.
- Generation costs are paid by the client: Taimur works on the client's Higgsfield (he covers Claude himself).
- Volume: no cap. Taimur outsources if work goes past his own capacity (up to 10 a day himself).
- First step for new leads: a free test ad on their Higgsfield.
- Work terms: contract first (per video or a monthly batch at the same rate). Full-time only if the offer is really good.

## 5. Price

$50 per video. A monthly batch is the same rate times the count (for example, 20 videos is $1,000). Quote in the call or after the free test, not in the first DM.

## 6. Content

Origin story for posts: seven years animating in Blender, now AI; 70 reels in a week for CheerMyRun; 130K organic on one. Post plan: docs/x-content.md.
