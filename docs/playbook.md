# AI Forge playbook

## Positioning

Volume shops (Doublespeed and similar) sell output: lots of AI clips, UGC and faceless content. AI Forge sells finished animation that holds attention, at a pace that suits social. The edge you can prove is seven years as a 3D animator: you write the story, you see bad timing and off-model frames, and you fix them in Blender instead of rerolling.

One line: **Story-first animated shorts by a 3D animator, produced with an AI pipeline so a series can ship every week.**

Keep reference names (Pixar, Zach D Films) in conversations and briefs. Don't put them on the site as claims.

## The proof gap (build this before applying)

Right now the site has no animation clips. Every claim on it needs a clip behind it. Build these six, in this order:

| # | Piece | What it proves | Length |
|---|-------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | "What if" explainer, 9:16 | Hook in 2 seconds, retention, the Zach D lane | 45 to 60s |
| 2 | Character brand film for a real product (spec, label it "spec") | You can sell with story; ties to your CGI Forge clients | 30s |
| 3 | Series episodes 1, 2, 3 with one recurring character | "At scale": same model, same world across shorts | 30 to 45s each |
| 4 | Widescreen cut of piece 1 | You can deliver 16:9 for YouTube or a website | 60 to 90s |
| 5 | Breakdown clip: beat board → raw generation → your Blender fix → final | Your actual edge over prompt-only creators | 20 to 30s |
| 6 | Showreel from the best 2 to 3 seconds of each | Hero video, first thing anyone sees | 20 to 40s |

Log real turnaround time per piece (brief to final). If episode 3 took two days, that's a number you can put on the site. Don't estimate one.

Spec work for a real brand: label it spec in the title or summary, and don't imply they paid for it.

Post pieces 1 and 3 on TikTok, Reels and Shorts as you finish them. Real view counts are the strongest proof for this lane, and they're yours to quote once they exist.

## Where the work comes from

- **Brands**: DTC brands that already run video ads. Your CGI Forge pipeline (wellness, nutrition, beverage) is the warmest start, since a character film is the natural upsell after renders.
- **Channels and creators**: faceless explainer channels, kids' and edu channels that need volume with a consistent character.
- **Agencies and content studios**: white-label animation for agencies selling short-form to their clients.
- **Roles**: "AI animator", "AI video artist", "3D animator (AI pipeline)", "creative technologist", content studio roles. Strong-economy markets, remote.

## Message templates

Adjust per lead. One observation must be true and countable for that brand or channel.

### Brand (cold)

> **Subject:** A 30s character spot for [Product]
>
> Your feed runs [N] product videos in the last month and none of them use a character or a story.
>
> Made you one. It doubles as an ad too.
>
> I run AI Forge, we make story-first animated shorts for brands. I've worked as a 3D animator for seven years.
>
> Worth a quick call to see if a series fits?

Only use "none of them use a character" if that's true after checking the full feed. For a brand with strong video, the honest angle: "Your product video is strong. The missing piece is a character people follow from post to post."

### Channel or creator

> **Subject:** Animated version of [their video topic]
>
> Your last [N] uploads are live-action or stock footage. Your topic on [X] would work as a 3D short.
>
> Made you a 20s test.
>
> I run AI Forge, we make story-first animated shorts. Seven years as a 3D animator, so characters stay on model across episodes.
>
> Open to a call about a weekly series?

### Job or gig application (opening paragraph)

> I've worked as a 3D animator for seven years, mostly in Blender, and I now produce animated shorts with an AI pipeline. I write the script and beat board first, generate the shots, and fix timing and off-model frames by hand. My portfolio has [N] shorts, including a three-episode series with one recurring character: [link]. I also run CGI Forge, a product-visualization studio; our ad creatives for heyyy (UK wellness) averaged 5.8% CTR across 4 ads, peak 8.26%.

Change the title and the first line per role. The facts stay the same.

## Pricing

Not on the site. Quote on a call after scope. Shorts priced per finished piece, series priced per month by episode count. 50% deposit, 50% before final files. Quote first, and only discount if they push back for real.
