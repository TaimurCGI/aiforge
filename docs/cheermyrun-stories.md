Transcripts (faster-whisper, 27 Nov; lightly checked against on-screen captions):

**CMR02 Look Left (33s).** Grandma tries to record a cheer; grandpa says she'll hear it at mile 10. "She won't hear me. I'll go." "You don't leave the house." At mile 10: "Emma! Look left!" "Grandma?" Grandpa: "Two hours in the car! He drives so slow!" "She said shouting into the phone wasn't loud enough." "You never leave the house." "For you, I did."

**CMR03 Nobody's Coming (31s).** "So, who's coming to watch on Sunday?" Everyone has an excuse ("Rosie's got a party"). "No, it's fine, honestly." That night each family member records a cheer ("Is this thing on?" "It's Dad, proud of you."). At the finish the whole family is there: "You said you were busy!" "We were! Busy recording!"

**CMR06 Kate's Photo (36s).** "Mommy, why are you never in the pictures?" "Someone has to hold the phone." The night before her first race the kids record a cheer: "She'll hear it in her headphones while she runs. Which mile?" "The one with the camera. Mile 11." At mile 11: "Smile for the camera!" Photo on the wall: "Now you're in the pictures."

**CMR07 Next Time (30s).** "Run the 5K with me." "Next time." "The half?" "Next time." "The marathon?" "Definitely next time." At mile 22 his cheer: "I always say next time. So I signed us both up for the spring half." "He's never ran in his life. Are those running shoes?" "I'm committed now."

**CMR10 Dad Doesn't Do Voice Messages (41s).** "Twelve people sent me cheers. Dad didn't." "You know your father. He doesn't do voice messages." Cheers 1 to 12 play along the course. Cheer 13 at mile 25: "It's Dad. I don't do these. I've been training since March. For one mile. This one." "You hate running." Dad (bib 421) runs the last mile with her.
