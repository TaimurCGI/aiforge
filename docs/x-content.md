## Strategy

### What works in this niche

1. **Receipts in line one.** A number the reader can check: views, cost, spend, result ("new top spender", "$16.93 to make", "3.4M views").
2. **Clip first, short caption.** The video carries the post; the caption is one or two lines plus the tool used.
3. **Direct questions to brand owners and editors.** "Genuine question..." and "are there better workflows?" pull replies, and replies push reach.
4. **Pain plus curiosity gap.** "Your AI UGC looks fake cause you're missing this extra step." Then the answer in the replies or a thread.
5. **Naming the trend with proof.** "Drama ads are printing" is everywhere; Taimur can back it with his own 600K+.
6. **Breakdown threads.** Numbered steps, a case study, a list of styles. These get saved and reposted.

### Taimur's edge, for the buyer

- **The USP is the combination** of rate, speed, quality and story (table above). Most AI editors in the hiring replies can show one of these; Taimur's posts should show all four, one number at a time.
- **Against "AI slop":** seven years as a 3D animator; he catches bad timing, weight and off-model frames. Higgsfield's Blender + AI film shows this audience cares about that pipeline.
- **Against "AI ads with no story":** he researches and writes every script; hiring posts ask for "scripts provided" or "understand scripts", so writing them himself is a step above.
- **Against "fake portfolios":** every number he posts links to a live Instagram post anyone can check.
- **Against "slow":** 70 reels in a week, up to 10 a day.
- **Range:** a pain relief cream (Ketro), a trading app (JTX) and a marathon cheer app (CheerMyRun). Story-first ads work for any product that sells online.
- **Localization data:** the same story in 7 languages with very different results. Few people post that.
- **Low-risk first step:** a free test ad on the brand's own Higgsfield, so they see his work on their product with their account before paying.
